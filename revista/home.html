Fuxicar

Fuxico é conversa de comadre e também o retalho costurado à mão que, junto com outros, vira colcha. Conta pra gente o que tava na tua mão e o que Deus fez com isso.In Brazil a fuxico is a chat between close friends, and also the hand-sewn patch that, joined with others, becomes a quilt. Tell us what was in your hand and what God did with it.Fuxico es charla de comadres y también el retazo cosido a mano que, junto a otros, se vuelve colcha.
